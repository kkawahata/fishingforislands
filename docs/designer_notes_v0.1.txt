Fishing For Islands — Build v0.1
=================================
April 2026

Play here: https://kkawahata.github.io/fishingforislands/
Open in Chrome or Firefox on desktop. Nothing to install.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
- Click ground to move, click NPCs/objects to interact
- WASD works too
- Right-drag to orbit camera, scroll to zoom, V to reset view
- E to interact, C to craft near a station
- Space to advance dialogue


DEV TOOLS (for testing)
-----------------------
Press the backtick key (`) to open the dev tools panel:
- Skip to any day
- Add items, unlock all islands, give yourself tools
- Makes it easy to jump ahead without replaying from Day 1


WHAT'S IMPLEMENTED
------------------
All 15 islands from the April spec, playable through Day 9+:

  Tier 1 (FTUE - Days 1-4):
  - Your Home: fire, crafting table, fishing rod, bed, house quests
  - Wheat Farm: dog fetch, shovel, magic wand, planting, harvest
  - Sheep Pasture: Sally, shearing, beds, fence building, sheep pregnancy
  - Fishing Pier: Maria, Old Man, fishing rod, cooking, fish catalogue

  Tier 2 (Early Game - Days 5-8):
  - Bakery: Pierre, mouse chase (5 islands), welcome dinner, cellar (Flame Stone)
  - Dense Forest: Ashley, squirrel, logging camp, 100 acorns, winter event (Frost Stone)
  - Rocks: Timmy, seals, pristine shells, pearl requests, hidden quest (Shadow Stone)
  - Granary: Raven, crow, windmill, wheat-to-grain, repair quest
  - Market Square: 3 merchants, coins economy, daily requests

  Stubs (Days 6-9+):
  - Mermaid Cove / Faerie Woods (exclusive pair)
  - Auction House, Gold Mine, Mount Mystery
  - Each has Quest 1 playable

Game Systems:
- 6 fish types with day-parity probability (snapper odd days, tuna even, etc.)
- Max 10 fish per day
- Prerequisite-based island unlocks (day + required islands)
- Exclusive choice pairs: Sheep/Pier (Day 3/4), Mermaid/Faerie
- Multi-day quests (sheep pregnancy, Theodore's requests, pearl requests)
- Mouse chase (moves to farthest island when caught)
- Winter event (snow cosmetics + snowman + Frost Stone)
- Hidden quest (3 Golden Trout -> Shadow Stone)
- 13 crafting recipes across 5 stations
- Magic Wand as catalyst for wood tier conversions
- Coins economy (buy/sell at Market Square)
- 5 magical stones collectible for endgame
- Auto-save each day in browser storage


WHAT'S PLACEHOLDER
------------------
- All art is colored 3D blocks (no real models or textures yet)
- Dialogue is minimal (vibes, not full scripts)
- No sound or music
- Economy has no real balancing (1:1 placeholder prices)
- Stub islands have only Quest 1
- No tree growth visuals yet (Dense Forest)
- Acorn island effect not spawning across islands yet
