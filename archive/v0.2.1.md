# v0.2.1 — Isolated turtle on a shortened dock
Date: 2026-04-22

## Summary
The turtle no longer has an island. The old size-12 platform is gone —
the turtle is now a free-floating body in the water, head facing east
toward a shortened dock that sticks out from home. The dock is half the
length of the previous bridge (4 units vs 8). The player walks to the
tip of the dock and interacts with the turtle from there.

## Changes

- `src/data.js` — Removed `turtle` from `ISLAND_GRID` and `ISLANDS`.
  `getTurtleBridgeBand()` rewritten to return a fixed dock geometry
  extending west from home's edge (length 4, the overlap into home is 1
  unit). New `getTurtleBodyPos()` helper returns where the turtle floats
  in the water. Turtle remains in `ISLAND_UNLOCK_RULES`, so
  `unlockedIslands.has('turtle')` still gates dock+turtle visibility.
- `src/World.js` — Removed `_addTurtleIsland` and the `isTurtleIsland`
  branch of `_createIsland`. Added `_buildTurtle()` which places the
  turtle body, shell, head (facing east), and flippers as a standalone
  group at `getTurtleBodyPos()`. Head stays in `interactables` but is no
  longer pushed to `npcMeshes` — the whole group bobs in `animateNPCs`
  via a new loop, so the generic NPC bob doesn't fight with the group's
  world offset. Dead `case 'turtle'` arm removed from `_createNPC`.
- `src/Player.js` — Added stuck detection to click-to-move. When the
  target is unreachable (e.g. clicking the turtle head in the water),
  the player walks as far as the dock tip, the next step makes no
  progress, and the walk cancels itself. That lets Game's pending
  arrival check fire the turtle interaction from the dock tip (distance
  to head ≈ 0.8 units, well under the 2.5-unit arrival threshold).
- `package.json`, `src/version.js` — bumped to `0.2.1`.
