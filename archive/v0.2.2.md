# v0.2.2 — Giant turtle (4x scale)
Date: 2026-04-22

## Summary
The turtle is now four times larger in every dimension. The body center
is repositioned further west so the (scaled) head still lands just off
the dock tip — parameterized against `TURTLE_SCALE` and the head's
local offset so future scale changes don't need manual re-tuning of the
body position. Bob amplitude scales with the turtle; rotation stays
constant in angle (which already reads as a bigger sway at scale).

At 4x, the shell rises to about `y=5.4` in world, making the turtle
visibly tower over the home platform — intentional, reads as a
sea-creature the size of the map rather than a pet.

## Geometry check
- Body center: `band.minX - 1 - 2.2 * 4 = -8.8`
- Head world position: `(0, 1.1, 16)` — 1 unit west of the dock tip
- Player reach from dock tip `(1, 0.5, 16)` to head = 1.0 unit (well
  under the 2.5-unit pending-interaction arrival threshold and the
  2.2-unit `E`-key interaction range).

## Changes
- `src/data.js` — new exported `TURTLE_SCALE` constant (4). Replaced
  the flat `TURTLE_OFFSET_FROM_DOCK` with a derived expression using
  `TURTLE_HEAD_LOCAL_X * TURTLE_SCALE + TURTLE_HEAD_DOCK_GAP` so head
  placement tracks any future scale change.
- `src/World.js` — `_buildTurtle` applies `group.scale.setScalar(TURTLE_SCALE)`.
  Bob vertical amplitude is `0.06 * TURTLE_SCALE` so motion stays
  perceptually proportional at any size.
- `package.json`, `src/version.js` — bumped to `0.2.2`.
